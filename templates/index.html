ABOUT PHISHGUARD

Understanding website threats

PhishGuard explores how machine-learning techniques can help identify URLs associated with phishing. The system analyses a submitted URL and presents model predictions to support further investigation.

◎

Our Project

Phishing websites can imitate legitimate online services to trick people into revealing passwords, financial information or other sensitive data. PhishGuard analyses website URLs and provides predictions to help users make informed decisions. Its output is a research aid, not a guarantee that a website is safe or malicious.

◈

Machine-Learning Models

The project compares three models. Their individual predictions can be reviewed during website inspection.

Logistic Regression
